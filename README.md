# City Cab Connect

Create a modern, high-converting website for a local cab service business in India.



Business Name: [Client Name Cab Service]

City: [City Name]



Goal:

Generate maximum direct bookings and WhatsApp enquiries from Google visitors.



Design Style:

- Clean, professional, mobile-first design

- Use trust elements like ratings, reviews, and real photos

- Colors: Black + Yellow (taxi theme) or premium dark theme



Sections Required:



1. Hero Section:

- Headline: "Book Your Cab in Minutes"

- Subheadline: "Fast, Reliable & Affordable Cab Service in [City]"

- CTA Buttons: "Book on WhatsApp" and "Call Now"



2. Quick Booking Form:

- Pickup location

- Drop location

- Date & Time

- Submit → redirects to WhatsApp with pre-filled message



3. Services:

- Local Ride

- Airport Transfer

- Outstation Trips



4. Why Choose Us:

- 24/7 Service

- Experienced Drivers

- Affordable Pricing

- On-time Pickup



5. Customer Reviews:

- Add 3–5 testimonials with 5-star style



6. Google Map Integration:

- Show business location



7. Sticky WhatsApp Button (Very Important)



8. Call Button (Mobile visible always)



9. Footer:

- Contact details

- WhatsApp number

- Quick links



Extra Features:

- Fast loading

- SEO optimized for "cab service in [City]"

- Mobile optimized (very important)



Main Focus:

Convert visitors into WhatsApp enquiries and direct calls.
